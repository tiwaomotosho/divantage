# 23 — Feature Completeness and App Scope

**Version:** 2.6 · **Date:** 21 October 2026 · **Status:** Governing document for "is the app level with everything the owner has asked for?"

## 1. Why this document exists
The owner asked that the app be "level to all the features I have shared". Until V2.4 those features lived in different documents, added one version at a time. This document lists each feature once, in plain words that stay close to what the owner said, and shows where it is specified, how it is tested and when it ships. It is checked by a script, so it cannot quietly fall behind.

## 2. The rule
1. **Release 1 is every row of the matrix in §4.** Nothing the owner has asked for is left to a later release, apart from the items in §6.
2. Each row has a **stage**:
   - **Beta:** on in the private beta (D-062).
   - **Launch:** built behind a feature flag during the beta and **on** at the public launch.
   - **Post-MVP:** deliberately later (D-050, D-045).
3. Each flag in `spec-assets/config/feature-flags.json` has a `launch` value, the value it must have in production at the public launch. Only `noindex_site`, `beta_banner` and `suggest_email` launch OFF (the first two are beta-only; the email field waits for the privacy notice, D-027).
4. **The check:** `python3 -I spec-assets/validate_feature_matrix.py .` fails when a row cites a decision, requirement, screen, test or flag that does not exist, when a shipping feature has no test, or when a shipping feature's flag does not launch ON. It runs in CI (TC-130) and is part of the public-launch gate (FR-073).
5. **Adding a feature:** a new owner request becomes a row in `spec-assets/qa/feature-matrix.csv`, with a decision in 02, a requirement in 01, a screen in 04 where there is one, and a test in 15. Run `bash spec-assets/generators/rebuild_all.sh` to refresh the table below.

## 5. What "level" means for each kind of feature
- **Built and tested:** a screen or behaviour with an acceptance test (most rows).
- **Content-dependent:** the feature works, but its value depends on content that people still need to write and check:
  - full guides for the 21 pilot tools exist as AI drafts and need a human check;
  - the 19 Entrepreneur tools without a full guide (22 §5.4) show the "Guide in progress" label until written;
  - every identity, need, roadmap and lens carries its expert-validation status (D-045).
- **Decision-dependent:** the name and domain (D-019), launch markets and legal text (D-027), the uptime monitor (D-018) and the licences (D-061) gate the **public launch**, not the build or the beta.
- **Judgement-dependent:** "very visual and sleek" cannot be proven by a test alone. TC-131 covers layout and contrast; a designer or the owner signs off the look at the end of Milestone D (17).

## 6. Deliberately not in the MVP
| Item | Where it stands |
|---|---|
| User accounts and synced progress | R2 (D-004). Progress and helpers work on the device without an account. |
| Bookmarks and curated collections | R1.5 (D-005, D-029) |
| AI chat or AI-assisted discovery | R2 (D-006) |
| Learning paths with certificates, projects | R2 (D-030) and doc 19 |
| Monetisation | After the MVP (D-050, doc 19). No ads, affiliates or paid placement before then. |
| Translating the interface into other languages | Later (01 §11.1). *Translation tools* are already a universal need for every profession. |
| Expert-validation badges | Post-MVP (D-045). The status label is shown from day one. |
| ESCO job-title aliases | Post-launch (D-049) |

## 7. Build order that gets to "level" fastest
Follow doc 17. In short:
1. Day 1: **Spike S-1** (11, ADR-008) to confirm the stack's build time, bundle size and security settings.
2. Weeks 2–4: the slice for the three helpers (22 §5): home, toolkit, tool, roadmap, **helpers and the helper panel**, Suggest an edit, What's new, flags, previews and rollback.
3. Week 5: private beta with friends.
4. Weekly after that: turn on the **Launch** rows one flag at a time (Explore-by tabs, helping mode and cards, Contributors, admin report), each with its tests.
5. Public launch when the gate in 15 §4 passes **and** `validate_feature_matrix.py` is clean.

## 8. Honest limits
- The matrix proves the **specification** covers every request. It does not prove the app built from it behaves correctly. That is what the tests in 15 are for.
- Counts in the pack (identities, tools, roadmaps) are regenerated from source files and can change. The matrix cites ids, not counts.
- The AI-drafted content is published under the owner's blanket approval (D-044) and labelled. A human check is still needed before anyone relies on prices, features or advice.
