# Pilot round 2: feedback form (changes from round 1 in bold)

**Recruit:** 15–20 people. At least 10 on mid-range Android, at least 6 "not very confident" with digital tools, at least 8 outside office jobs. Re-invite 5 people from round 1.

## About you
1. Your job or role (free text)
2. Which describes you? Employed / Self-employed / Student / Looking for work / Retired / Prefer not to say
3. Device: Android phone / iPhone / Tablet / Laptop or desktop · **Phone model (optional)**
4. Confidence with digital tools: Not confident / A little / Fairly / Very
5. **In one sentence, before you start: what do you think this site is for?** (asked after 10 seconds on the home page)

## Task 1: find tools for your job (role, needs)
6. Did you manage it? Yes easily / Yes with difficulty / No, I got stuck · **If stuck, where?**
7. Was your job in the list? Exactly / Close match / No · **If no, what did you type?**
8. Do the needs match your work? Yes mostly / Partly / No · **Which one did not fit?**

## Task 2: start learning one tool
9. Did you manage it? (as above) · **Which button did you use?**
10. The learning path felt written for my kind of work (agree scale) · **Why?**
11. **Did you notice the label on the path (For your job / In depth / General guide)? What did it tell you?**

## Task 3: use a helper or kit
12. Did you manage it? (as above)
13. Was the helper bar useful? Yes / Didn't see it / Didn't understand it / Got in the way · **Did the tip explain it?**

## After the tasks
14. I know what my next step would be (agree scale) · **What is it?**
15. I trust the information on this site (agree scale) · **What would make you trust it more?**
16. The site looks modern and trustworthy (agree scale)
17. It was easy to use (agree scale)
18. I learned about at least one tool I didn't know (agree scale) · **Which?**
19. Did anything break or look wrong? (multi-select + screenshot upload)
20. How likely are you to recommend it to a friend or colleague? 0–10 · **What is the main reason for your score?**
21. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
22. **Would you use it again next week? Yes / Maybe / No · For what?**
23. Can we contact you again? Yes / No
