=lens spreadsheets x financial-modelling | Excel for financial modelling
for: financial-analyst, fpa-analyst, investment-banker, financial-modeller, private-equity-associate, cfo, startup-founder, accountant, equity-research-analyst, project-finance-analyst, real-estate-analyst
why: Almost every valuation, budget and investment decision is first built in a spreadsheet model. A clear, auditable model is how finance people earn trust.
different: Financial modellers live in the keyboard, build three linked statements, separate inputs from calculations, and obsess over checks and sign conventions. Charts matter less than integrity, flexibility and speed.
priority: xl-formulas-1, xl-model-design, xl-logic, xl-what-if, xl-dates-text, xl-dynamic-arrays, xl-conditional, xl-power-query
+beginner | Keyboard-first working | Navigate and build without the mouse | shortcuts for navigation, selection, fill and formatting; consistent colour coding (inputs blue, formulas black) | Rebuild a small table using only the keyboard
+intermediate | Three-statement model | Link income statement, balance sheet and cash flow | revenue and cost drivers; working capital; depreciation schedule; debt schedule; balance check | Build a five-year three-statement model for a simple business
+intermediate | Time-series and timing flags | Model monthly, quarterly and annual periods correctly | period headers; flags for start/end dates; EOMONTH; annualising | Add a construction period and an operating period flag
+expert | Valuation | Value a business or project | discounted cash flow; XNPV/XIRR; terminal value; WACC inputs; comparable multiples | Value the model's business with a DCF and sensitivity table
+expert | Scenarios and model review | Stress-test and review a model like an auditor | scenario switches; sensitivity tornado; circularity handling; model audit checklist | Add base/upside/downside scenarios and review another person's model
project: Build a three-statement model and DCF for a small business (for example, a bakery or a solar mini-grid) with three scenarios and a one-page dashboard.
pitfalls: Hard-coding numbers inside formulas ; Mixing inputs and calculations on the same sheet ; No balance-sheet check ; Sign errors between cash flow and balance sheet ; Circular references hidden with iterative calculation
pairs: microsoft-powerpoint, power-bi, python, think-cell
