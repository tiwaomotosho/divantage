# Goal safety rules (D-087). A goal gets every tag whose keywords appear (as whole words or word starts) in its slug, label or search words.
# tag | Label | keywords (comma) | What to know (one or two sentences, plain language) | Red flags (semicolon separated) | Where to get help
jobs | Job and income offers | job, jobs, hire, hiring, recruiter, freelance, freelancer, internship, intern, vacancy, job interview, remote job, remote work, scholarship, fellowship, apply for | Real employers and real scholarships do not charge you to apply or to be hired. Check the company and the person before you share documents or bank details. | asks you to pay for a form, training or equipment first; interview only on WhatsApp or Telegram; salary far above the usual; a cheque or transfer 'to buy equipment'; urgent deadline | Search the company on its official site and on CAC records, then report scams on EFCC's Eagle Eye page
money | Money, investing and payments | invest, investing, investment, loan, loans, lending, savings, forex, trading, crypto, funding, grant, grants, money transfer, remittance, payment, payments, payout, bank account, banking, wallet, microfinance, get paid | Money tools can lose you money. Check that the provider is licensed in your country and read the fees before you send anything. Nothing here is financial advice. | promises of fixed or doubled returns; pressure to recruit others; withdrawal fees before you can cash out; app that asks for your contacts and photos; unlicensed lender | SEC Nigeria and CBN lists for licensed providers; EFCC for fraud reports
kids | Children and teaching | kids, kid, child, children, pupil, pupils, teach kids, classroom, parental, parents of | Children's data and images are sensitive. Use child-safe platforms, avoid sharing identifying details and follow your school's rules and the law on children's data. | an app that asks for a child's full name, photo or location; open chat with strangers; ads aimed at children; sharing children's work publicly without consent | Take It Down for removing images; your school's safeguarding lead; the Nigeria Data Protection Commission
health | Health information | health advice, health information, health worker, health record, health emergenc, patient, patients, doctor, symptom, symptoms, medicine, drug, drugs, clinic, medical, nursing, therapy, nutrition | These tools give information, not a diagnosis. For anything serious or urgent, see a qualified health worker or go to the nearest hospital. | a tool that diagnoses with certainty; a cure with no side effects; selling prescription drugs without a prescription | A licensed doctor or pharmacist; NAFDAC to check a product
legal | Contracts, registration and tax | contract, contracts, legal, lawyer, register a business, registration, tax, taxes, compliance, privacy policy, terms and conditions, ndpa, cac, copyright | Templates and AI drafts are a starting point. Have a lawyer or the official body confirm anything that binds you or your business, because rules change. | an agent who wants cash to 'speed up' registration; a copied contract you do not understand; ignoring a filing deadline | The official portal for the agency concerned; a qualified lawyer or accountant
privacy | Privacy and accounts | password, passwords, privacy, data, security, secure, vpn, account, accounts, login, hacked, scan, ocr, upload, backup | Do not upload private documents (IDs, contracts, medical records) to free online tools you do not know. Use two-step sign-in on important accounts. | a site that asks for your password or OTP; a link sent by an unknown contact; a free tool that wants broad permissions | Your account provider's recovery page; NDPC for your data rights
ai | Using AI tools | ai, chatgpt, claude, gemini, copilot, chatbot, generate, generative, assistant | AI can be wrong or invent facts and sources. Check anything that matters, do not paste private or customer data, and follow your school or employer's rules on AI use. | invented references; confident wrong answers; sharing personal data in a prompt | The tool's own privacy settings; your school or employer's AI policy
official-fees | Government and exam services | passport, visa, nin number, nin slip, bvn, waec, neco, jamb, driving licence, driver's licence, tax id, tin number, immigration | Use the official site or office only. Official fees are published, and agents who ask for extra money may be scammers. | someone offers to 'fast-track' for cash; a lookalike website; fees paid to a personal account | The official agency website and offices; report extortion to the relevant agency
content | Copyright and posting | video, videos, music, song, songs, photo, photos, image, images, podcast, thumbnail, stream, download, creator | Only use music, photos and clips you made or have a licence for, or you can lose reach or income to copyright strikes. Check the licence on free libraries, and get consent before posting anyone's face or voice. | 'free' downloads of paid songs or films; cracked editing software that carries malware; posting someone's photo without asking | The platform's copyright help page; the licence terms on the source site
