# DIGITAL ADVANTAGE — Product & Design Specification Pack
**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** 21 October 2026 · **Status:** Foundational product blueprint

## Purpose
This pack defines Digital Advantage as a global, mobile-first, responsive digital discovery and learning platform that helps people use technology to compete, grow, and scale. It is written to align product owners, designers, developers, content editors, and AI-assisted builders around one coherent implementation.

## Product north star
Help a person move from **“I need to do something”** to **“I know which tools and methods to use, how to use them, and what to do next.”**

## Important scope note
This is a comprehensive foundational specification, not a claim that every future feature or every tool record has already been researched. Product decisions that depend on implementation budget, hosting, legal review, analytics policy, or user research are marked as decisions to validate. Do not fabricate tool prices, product features, or external URLs. Verify current facts at publication time.

## Non-negotiable principles
- Mobile-first, responsive, fast, accessible, and easy to navigate.
- The platform is larger than its originating class or conference.
- Users can browse the public knowledge library without creating an account.
- Search and browsing must work without generative AI.
- AI is an optional helper, not a source of unverified truth.
- Every tool, guide, task, workflow, and learning path has a shareable canonical URL.
- Prioritise practical outcomes over raw catalogue size.
- Make free, freemium, trial, and paid plans clearly distinct.
- Show provenance and review dates for changeable claims.
- Build a content model that can expand without redesigning the site.
